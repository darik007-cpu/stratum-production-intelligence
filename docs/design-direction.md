# STRATUM — industrial field operations

The current direction makes the oil product visibly different from the separate banking workspace. STRATUM is a dark engineering workbench: a persistent module rail, compact technical header, dense operational readouts, a large 3D field and a selected-well inspector. The previous ivory editorial composition and oversized generic headlines have been replaced.

## Product structure

The module rail names the work directly: Operations brief, Field overview, Well diagnostics, Export planning, Review actions, Data and Regional map. At viewport widths of 1,000 px or less it becomes visible, horizontally scrollable navigation. It does not hide navigation in an off-screen menu. The compact header retains data provenance, asset search and the review queue.

The operations brief starts with the actual problem: production shortfalls and export constraints. A procedural field model sits beside the engineering review sequence. A compact instrument strip shows synthetic field totals, and four module launchers connect to the working tools.

Field overview places production readouts first, then the interactive field and a well inspector side by side. The inspector includes oil rate, pressure, pump load and water cut. Below, the exception register and production trend support investigation and export planning. Diagnostics, import and review pages use the same dark technical panels and smaller, descriptive headings.

The independent Regional map is public geographic context. It does not assign real locations to the synthetic well model. Its geography, source information and public operator context remain distinct from well measurements.

## Visual language

- Graphite and dark petrol backgrounds distinguish the product from banking's light ledger workspace.
- Warm amber marks selected modules, primary actions and production readouts. Cyan, muted green and coral keep measurements and status categories distinguishable.
- Monospaced engineering readouts emphasize units and numbers. Plain text labels replace editorial slogans.
- Compact squared controls, panel dividers, procedural 3D equipment and numbered modules create an operations-console character.
- The 3D terrain uses slate and steel tones; warm machinery details remain readable without changing signal classifications.

## Reference history

The earlier design exploration considered [Porsche](https://www.porsche.com/), [Apple](https://www.apple.com/), [OpenAI](https://openai.com/) and [NVIDIA](https://www.nvidia.com/). The current layout retains object focus, clear actions and a substantial technical stage, while adopting a distinct petroleum-operations composition. No brand assets, logos or copied marketing text are used.

## Behavior and limits

All model controls, CSV validation and export, scenario inputs, mass-balance calculations, local review persistence, search focus management and error feedback remain available. The stylesheet changes presentation; the domain calculations are unchanged. Simulated data and imported snapshots remain explicitly identified. There is no live operational connection, equipment dispatch or verified failure prediction.
